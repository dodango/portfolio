05Mechanical design · FEA

Generative-design clarinet thumb rest

ME 108, Winter 2026, with Ryan Lee

3.086 g
final mass in AlSi10Mg
0.005 mm
peak displacement under a 25 N load, against a 0.1 mm limit
23.6 MPa
peak von Mises stress, against 240 MPa yield

A redesign of the clarinet thumb rest using Autodesk Fusion Generative Design, with a wider offset ledge meant to spread load away from the thumb joint. The objective was minimum mass, subject to a 0.1 mm displacement limit and a safety factor of at least 2. The loads were a 20 N vertical load and a 5 N lateral load on the ledge, with fixed constraints at the two screw standoffs and sliding constraints against the clarinet body.

We ran three iterations, at 4.119 g, 2.022 g, and a final 3.086 g. The 2.022 g version was the lightest, but its small footprint would have concentrated pressure on the wooden body and risked tearing out the screws, so we changed to a full backplate to spread the contact load. The report stops at simulation, with a printed prototype listed as future work.
