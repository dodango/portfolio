Laziness Detector — ME 10

ME 10: Engineering Graphics & Design, Spring 2024

15
line items in the assembly’s bill of materials

The Laziness Detector is an Arduino-based study accountability device that monitors whether someone remains at their workstation for a preset period and alerts them if they leave early.

I developed the device from initial sketches through cardboard prototyping, CAD, and final assembly, combining mechanical design, wiring, and Arduino programming.

The build uses an Arduino Nano, two IR sensors, three indicator LEDs, a piezo buzzer, a potentiometer, a push button, and a 9 V battery. The electronics sit on two breadboards within a stepped enclosure made from cardboard and 3D-printed components.
