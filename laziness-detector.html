Laziness Detector — ME 10

ME 10: Engineering Graphics & Design, Spring 2024

15
line items in the assembly’s bill of materials

The Laziness Detector is a study-time accountability device designed to keep someone at their desk for an allotted period. It uses sensors to detect whether the person stays at the workstation and alerts them if they leave early.

I developed it from concept sketches through a cardboard prototype and CAD model, then built a working Arduino device combining mechanical design, wiring, and code. The enclosure uses cardboard and 3D-printed parts.

The assembly is an Arduino Nano with two IR sensors, three LEDs, a piezo buzzer, a potentiometer, a button, and a 9V battery, mounted on two breadboards inside a stepped enclosure with a base plate and front cover.
