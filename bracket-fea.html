07Structural analysis

Front wing bracket — FEA study

ME 108 · GR26 front wing mounting bracket · With Ryan Lee

41.08 MPa
peak von Mises stress under 230 N vertical and 46 N horizontal loads
0.364 mm
maximum displacement
6.7
approximate factor of safety against yield, 6061-T6 aluminum

In a separate structural analysis study, we evaluated a GR26 aluminum front wing mounting bracket using SolidWorks Simulation. The baseline load case applied 230 N vertically and 46 N horizontally to assess stress concentrations and displacement around the mounting geometry.

The reported static analysis yielded a peak von Mises stress of 41.08 MPa and maximum displacement of 0.364 mm, with an approximate factor of safety of 6.7 against yield for 6061-T6 aluminum. The report also investigates mesh convergence and a deliberately severe 300 N lateral-load case, representing a worst-case side impact intended to drive the original bracket beyond its yield limit and assess how a revised fillet could improve its strength.
