07Structural analysis

Front wing bracket — FEA study

ME 108 · GR26 front wing mounting bracket · With Ryan Lee

41.08 MPa
peak von Mises stress under 230 N vertical and 46 N horizontal loads
0.364 mm
maximum displacement
6.7
approximate factor of safety against yield, 6061-T6 aluminum

In a separate structural analysis study, we evaluated an aluminum GR26 front-wing mounting bracket using SolidWorks Simulation. The baseline load case applied 230 N vertically, determined from predicted aerodynamic downforce, and 46 N horizontally to assess stress concentrations and displacement around the mounting geometry.

The static analysis yielded a peak von Mises stress of 41.08 MPa and maximum displacement of 0.364 mm, corresponding to an approximate factor of safety of 6.7 against yielding in 6061-T6 aluminum. We also investigated mesh convergence and a deliberately severe 300 N lateral load, representing a hypothetical worst-case side impact. This loading was intended to push the original bracket beyond yielding and assess how increasing the fillet radius could reduce stress concentrations and improve its strength.
